Money

Every lorry's payment: when, and how much

The पैसा (Money) tab opens on what is due to you as of today. Below it, the next 7 days — how much falls due on each day. And every lorry on its own line: the amount, the date it was due, and how many days late.

  • Received this month

    A month of money in, at a glance.

  • Due · Late · Received

    One tap sorts out which lorry’s money is stuck where.

  • Late? Call the office

    Tap “Ask the office” on a late lorry and the call goes through. Once paid, the bank slip is right there.

Just ask Madam. By voice.

Madam is the app's voice assistant. Press the mic and speak, or type — she tells you where your lorry is, which papers are pending and what money is due, sends a paper for you, and can even record a new deal.

  • A deal needs your “yes”

    Before she records a new deal, Madam asks you to say “yes”.

  • The rate comes from the rate card

    A deal's rate always comes from today's rate card — never from what was heard.

  • In your language

    Madam answers in whatever language the app is set to.

Questions

Common questions

How is freight worked out?

From the road distance between your place and the mill — it is an estimate, not a fixed amount. You can change your place any time.

How do I know when I will be paid?

The पैसा (Money) tab shows every lorry’s amount and due date, and the next 7 days. If a payment is late, call the office from right there.

Does the driver have to do anything?

No. How far a lorry has got is read from its papers. The driver needs no app.

Who is Madam?

The app's voice assistant. Press and speak, or type — she answers in the app's language. She asks your permission the first time.

How does my level go up?

From the lorries that reached the mill in the last 90 days, and sending each factory receipt within 2 days. Silver at 10 lorries, Gold at 25, Platinum at 60.

Does the app cost anything?

No. The app is free for brokers and suppliers trading with OSHT.

What if there is no signal?

The photo of the paper waits on the phone, and goes by itself the moment signal returns.

Is it on iPhone too?

Yes — on both Google Play and the App Store, as “OSHT - Agro Trading”.
